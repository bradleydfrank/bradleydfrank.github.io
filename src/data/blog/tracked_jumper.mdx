This was taken while working on [this paper](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adma.202506100). This video is a little bit of a mystery, a Janus droplet experiences a saturated solution of surfactant which can partition into the hydrocarbon oil phase fast enough that it appears to jump at the same time as it actuates non-spherically. Does it jump, or is it a small Maraongi flow?
