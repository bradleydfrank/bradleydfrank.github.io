This is a collection of simulations for ray tracing simulations made in the paper below. In this, "dye emission" is placed evenly in the hydrocarbon (red) phase of the droplet, and the profile of emission is tracked. 

See more about this project on the [poster](/posts/poster-droptics/) I made for it, and the paper below:

[__Morphology‐Directed Light Emission from Fluorescent Janus Colloids for Programmable Chemical‐To‐Optical Signal Transduction__](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adom.202300875) [(Poster)](/posts/poster-droptics/)   
__Bradley D Frank__, Sara Nagelberg, Agata W Baryzewska, Pablo Simón Marqués, Markus Antonietti, Mathias Kolle, Lukas Zeininger   
_Advanced Optical Materials_ 2023
